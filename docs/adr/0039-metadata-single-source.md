## Context

Self-hosted media stacks combine multiple specialized services that touch the same underlying media items:

- **SnatchArr** (`/ˈsnætʃ.ɑːr/`, from English *snatch* — to grab missing or cutoff-unmet releases — and the `-*arr` media-automation suffix): the rate-budgeted wanted-search scheduler and library-policy control plane (`api/`, `worker/`, `web/`).
- **Whisparr** (`/ˈwɪs.pɑːr/`, from English *whisper* and the `-*arr` suffix): the adult video manager across two active lineages — **Whisparr v2** (a **Sonarr**-derived site-and-episode hierarchy) and **Whisparr v3**, code-named **Eros** (`/ˈɪər.ɒs/`, after the Greek deity of attraction; a **Radarr**-derived scene-and-movie model).
- **Stash** (`/stæʃ/`, from English *stash* — a private store or collection; also referred to as **StashApp** `/ˈstæʃ.æp/`): the self-hosted local organizer and GraphQL server for adult video scenes, image galleries, performers, and file fingerprints.
- **stash-box** (`/ˈstæʃ.bɒks/`, a compound of *Stash* and *box* denoting a community metadata repository): the open-source GraphQL metadata engine powering community adult catalogs such as **StashDB** (`/ˈstæʃ.diː.biː/`, *Stash Database*) and creator-content instances ([ADR-0031: Creator content catalogued in FansDB inside the adult module](0031-creator-content-fansdb.md)).
- **ThePornDB** (`/ðə ˈpɔːrn.diː.biː/`, abbreviated **TPDB** `/ˌtiː.piː.diːˈbiː/`, named by analogy with *TheMovieDB* and *TheTVDB*): the community REST and stash-box-compatible metadata directory for adult scenes, movies, sites, and performers.
- **Jellyfin** (`/ˈdʒɛl.i.fɪn/`, from *jellyfish* and *fin*, originated as the community free-software fork of **Emby** `/ˈɛm.biː/`, itself named from *Media Browser*): the media server that renders libraries to end users and tracks per-user playback state.
- **Sonarr** (`/ˈsoʊ.nɑːr/`, blending *sonar* and `-*arr`, formerly *NzbDrone*): the television series manager backed by **TheTVDB** (`/ðə ˌtiː.viː.diːˈbiː/`, *The Television Database*, abbreviated **TVDb**) and **TMDb** (`/ˌtiː.ɛm.diːˈbiː/`, *The Movie Database*).
- **Radarr** (`/ˈreɪ.dɑːr/`, blending *radar* and `-*arr`, forked from Sonarr for cinema): the feature-film manager backed by TMDb and **IMDb** (`/ˌaɪ.ɛm.diːˈbiː/`, *Internet Movie Database*).
- **Lidarr** (`/ˈlaɪ.dɑːr/`, blending *lidar* and `-*arr`): the music collection manager backed by **MusicBrainz** (`/ˌmjuː.zɪkˈbreɪnz/`, an open music encyclopedia combining *music* and *brain*).
- **Readarr** (`/ˈriː.dɑːr/`, blending *read* and `-*arr`) and its maintained Readarr-class book-management forks **Chaptarr** (`/ˈtʃæp.tɑːr/`, from *chapter* and `-*arr`) and **Bookshelf** (`/ˈbʊk.ʃɛlf/`, a literal compound of *book* and *shelf*): book and audiobook managers backed by **Goodreads** (`/ˈɡʊd.riːdz/`), **OpenLibrary** (`/ˈoʊ.pən ˌlaɪ.brɛr.i/`), and **Hardcover** (`/ˈhɑːrd.kʌv.ər/`).
- **Seerr** (`/ˈsiː.ər/`, from English *seer* — one who looks ahead — unifying **Overseerr** `/ˌoʊ.vərˈsiː.ər/` and **Jellyseerr** `/ˈdʒɛl.i.siː.ər/`): the request-management front door.
- **Prowlarr** (`/ˈpraʊ.lɑːr/`, from *prowl* and `-*arr`): the indexer proxy and search aggregator.
- **Configarr** (`/kənˈfɪɡ.ɑːr/`, from *configure* and `-*arr`): the declarative synchronizer for `*arr` quality profiles and custom formats.
- **golusoris** (`/ˌɡoʊ.luːˈsɔːr.ɪs/`, from *Go* and Latin *lusoris*, genitive of *lusor* — "of the player/maker"), **goenvoy** (`/ˌɡoʊˈɛn.vɔɪ/`, from *Go* and *envoy* — a diplomatic messenger between services), and **sveltesentio** (`/ˌsvɛltˈsɛn.ti.oʊ/`, from **Svelte** `/svɛlt/` — slender — and Latin *sentio* — "I perceive"): the shared Go application framework (`github.com/golusoris/golusoris`), multi-service API client monorepo (`github.com/golusoris/goenvoy`), and accessible frontend toolkit (`@sveltesentio/*`) used by SnatchArr.

### The multi-refresh overwrite problem

Today, each service refreshes metadata on its own schedule from its own upstream provider or local `.nfo` (`/ˌɛn.ɛfˈoʊ/`, from *info* sidecar) files and overwrites fields that another service or the operator has already curated:

1. **Adult metadata clobbering across Whisparr, Stash, Jellyfin, and SnatchArr**: Whisparr v3 (`whisparr_v3`) refreshes scene records from a single configured stash-box or TPDB endpoint; Stash runs its own stash-box and scraper passes over the same video files; Jellyfin runs its own metadata providers or reads sidecar `.nfo` files written by Whisparr. When an operator splits a false-merged performer in Stash, fixes a studio code in SnatchArr, or locks a title in Jellyfin, the next scheduled refresh in a sibling tool overwrites the correction or re-writes the sidecar file.
2. **General media drift across `*arr`, Seerr, and Jellyfin**: Sonarr and Radarr own file naming, monitoring, and TMDb/TVDb acquisition metadata, while Jellyfin owns per-user playback progress (`UserData`) and local presentation overrides (`LockedFields`). When both `*arr` and Jellyfin write `.nfo` files or refresh artwork without a clear ownership boundary, custom posters, sort titles, or tags revert on rescan.
3. **Shipped baseline vs planned architecture in SnatchArr**:
   - **Shipped in code today**:
     - `*arr` instance registry and afterglow memory in `instances` and `processed_items` (`api/internal/store/migrations/postgres/000001_init.up.sql:8-69`).
     - Seerr request cache and TMDb/TVDb-to-`*arr` entity resolution in `seerr_requests` (`000004_seerr.up.sql:5-31`, `api/internal/seerr/sync.go:153-185`, `api/internal/arrclient/resolve.go:26-63`).
     - Jellyfin/Emby watch-state ingestion and TVDb-keyed season gating in `watchaware_sources`, `watchaware_item_watches`, and `watchaware_gate_series` (`000029_watchaware.up.sql:5-33`, `000031_watchaware_gate.up.sql:9-23`, `000034_watchaware_tvdb.up.sql:4`, `api/internal/watchaware/module.go:47-55`).
     - Jellyfin and proxy user identity mapping in `users.external_id` and `user_identities` (`000026_user_external_id.up.sql:5`, `000037_user_identities.up.sql:5-14`).
     - Adult module baseline (`000036_adult_module.up.sql:6-71`, `000038_adult_favorite_urls.up.sql:5-9`, `000039_adult_image_items_unique.up.sql:7-16`): stash-box endpoint registry (`adult_stashbox_instances`), favourite performers and studios with cross-endpoint foreign ID links (`adult_favorite_performers`, `adult_performer_links`, `adult_favorite_studios`, `adult_studio_links`), deterministic URL/ID merger (`api/internal/adult/merge.go:39-145`), periodic stash-box link sync (`api/internal/adult/sync.go:46-210`), and filesystem image-set scanner with SHA-256 item checksums (`adult_image_sets`, `adult_image_items`, `api/internal/adult/scan.go:45-463`).
   - **Planned (not yet shipped in code)**:
     - Normalized adult catalog (`adult_performers`, `adult_studios`, `adult_scenes`, `adult_movie_scenes`, `adult_tags`, `adult_external_refs`, `adult_scene_fingerprints`, `adult_field_provenance`, `adult_identity_events`, `adult_identity_exclusions`, `adult_identity_suggestions`) in [ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md).
     - Fellegi-Sunter probabilistic matching engine, per-field reconciliation (`ignore`, `merge`, `overwrite`), audit workbench, and privacy-fenced model connection in [ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md).
     - Jellyfin companion plugin (`lusoris/jellyfin-plugin-snatcharr`) and `/api/v1/adult/jellyfin/*` / `/api/v1/adult/metadata/scene` delivery endpoints in [ADR-0028](0028-jellyfin-plugin.md).
     - Stash inventory integration and `.stashignore` hand-off in [ADR-0032](0032-stash-own-or-integrate.md).
     - Cross-library community requests and voting (`requests` table) in [ADR-0036](0036-community-requests-and-voting.md).
     - Numbered and studio-scoped performer identities in ADR-0037 (proposed).

This ADR defines the single-source-of-truth contract across all of these systems: which system owns each field, which external IDs join records across boundaries, how synchronization runs and defaults to read-only, how conflicts and user locks resolve, how duplicates merge without ever matching on name alone, and how the stack degrades when Stash or Whisparr is absent.

## Decision

### 1. Field ownership

Every metadata and state attribute has exactly one **owning system** (the canonical authority where the reconciled truth lives), an explicit set of **readers**, and a restricted set of **writers**. Adult module rows ([ADR-0027](0027-adult-module.md), [ADR-0031](0031-creator-content-fansdb.md), [ADR-0032](0032-stash-own-or-integrate.md), [ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md), [ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md)) are separated from general media rows (`sonarr`, `radarr`, `lidarr`, `readarr`, `seerr`, `jellyfin`) because SnatchArr acts as the normalizing metadata hub for adult entities ([ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md)), whereas for general media the respective `*arr` instance and its upstream catalog (TMDb, TVDb, MusicBrainz, book providers) remain canonical for catalog metadata while SnatchArr owns only request routing, rate budgets, and watch-aware monitoring policy.

### 2. External IDs as the join key

Cross-system joins never rely on display titles or person names. Every join uses a typed external identifier or content hash. The table below maps each identifier family across systems and cites where SnatchArr stores or resolves it today versus in planned migrations.

### 4. Conflict rule, field-level provenance, and preserving user edits

When two or more sources provide values for the same entity and field, deterministic conflict resolution prevents oscillation and protects human edits.

#### 4a. Field-level provenance and lock model (Adult catalog, planned in [ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md) and [ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md))

Every reconcilable scalar and array field on `adult_performers`, `adult_studios`, and `adult_scenes` has a row in `adult_field_provenance` ([ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md)) keyed by `(entity_kind, entity_id, field_name)`:

- `winning_source_ref_id UUID REFERENCES adult_external_refs(id)`: the external source whose value currently populates the canonical column (or `NULL` when set directly by an operator).
- `value_sha256 CHAR(64)`: SHA-256 digest of the canonical normalized value currently stored.
- `candidate_values_json JSONB`: map of `{ "<external_ref_id>": { "value": ..., "observed_at": ..., "priority": ... } }` recording what every linked source most recently reported, so the UI can show diffs and revert without re-querying upstream.
- `locked_by_user BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT FALSE`: hard lock flag.
- `decided_by VARCHAR(16) NOT NULL`: `'priority'` | `'merge'` | `'user'` | `'model'`.

#### 4b. Precedence ladder and per-field reconciliation strategies

On every sync pass (`adult.Ticker`), each field `(entity_kind, entity_id, field_name)` is evaluated in strict precedence order (subject to the default policy choice in `OQ-0039-3`):

1. **Rule 1 — User lock wins unconditionally (`locked_by_user = TRUE`)**:
   If `locked_by_user = TRUE`, the canonical column on `adult_performers`, `adult_studios`, or `adult_scenes` is **never modified** by any automated sync pass, regardless of source priority or upstream timestamps. The incoming upstream value is recorded only inside `candidate_values_json` so the operator can inspect upstream drift in the audit workbench ([ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md)).
2. **Rule 2 — Non-empty guard (null/blank never clobbers populated data)**:
   An upstream `NULL`, empty string `""`, empty array `[]`, or zero integer `0` never overwrites a non-empty canonical value, even if the empty value comes from a higher-priority source ([ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md), [ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md)).
3. **Rule 3 — Strategy-specific resolution for unlocked fields (`locked_by_user = FALSE`)** ([ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md)):
   - **`ignore` (fill-only)**: If the canonical field is already non-empty, keep the existing value and record the new candidate in `candidate_values_json`. If the canonical field is empty, populate it from the highest-priority source that supplies a non-empty value.
   - **`merge` (set union for multi-value fields)**: Used by default for `aliases`, `urls`, `tags`, `external_refs`, and `fingerprints`. Values from all linked sources are normalized (case-folded/trimmed strings; `NormalizeURL` in `api/internal/adult/merge.go:19-37` for URLs) and merged via set union. Deletions made by an operator are recorded as tombstones in `candidate_values_json` with `locked_by_user = TRUE` so a subsequent `merge` pass does not re-insert a removed alias or tag.
   - **`overwrite` (ordered source priority list)**: Used by default for scalar fields (`title`, `details`, `release_date`, `duration`, `code`, `studio_id`, `birthdate`, `country`, physical attributes). Each source has an integer priority (`adult_stashbox_instances.priority` in `000036_adult_module.up.sql:10`, extended to TPDB, Whisparr, and Stash in [ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md); lower number = higher priority, e.g., priority `10` beats priority `20`). The non-empty value from the lowest-numbered priority source wins, updates `winning_source_ref_id` and `value_sha256`, and writes the canonical column.

#### 4c. How a user edit in Jellyfin, Stash, or SnatchArr survives the next refresh

1. **User edit in SnatchArr**:
   Editing any field or accepting a value in the SnatchArr UI / audit workbench sets `locked_by_user = TRUE` and `decided_by = 'user'` in `adult_field_provenance`. All future `adult.Ticker` refreshes leave the field untouched (Rule 1).
2. **User edit in Jellyfin**:
   When a user edits an item in the Jellyfin UI (for example, changing a movie/scene `Name`, `Overview`, `Genres`, `Tags`, `Studios`, or `People`), Jellyfin appends the modified field name to `BaseItemDto.LockedFields` (or sets `BaseItemDto.LockData = true` when locking the entire item).
   - **Adult items served by `lusoris/jellyfin-plugin-snatcharr` ([ADR-0028](0028-jellyfin-plugin.md))**: Jellyfin's metadata refresh pipeline checks `LockedFields` and `LockData` before applying fields returned by the plugin's `IRemoteMetadataProvider<Movie, MovieInfo>` implementation (ADR-0028). In addition, the companion plugin inspects `ItemUpdateType` / `LockedFields` before writing any field or image so locked fields in Jellyfin are never overwritten on refresh.
   - **General media items (`sonarr`, `radarr`, `lidarr`, `readarr`)**: `*arr` instances must be configured not to overwrite existing `.nfo` files on routine rescan when operators curate fields in Jellyfin, or Jellyfin's `LockedFields` acts as the barrier during Jellyfin library scans (`OQ-0039-5`, `OQ-0039-6`).
3. **User edit in Stash**:
   When Stash is integrated as an inventory and fingerprint source ([ADR-0032](0032-stash-own-or-integrate.md), [ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md)):
   - One write path is already decided: a confirmed performer split writes back to a connected Stash automatically (ledger `snatcharr-oq-0037-2`, ADR-0037 (proposed)). Every other write-back to Stash is open (`OQ-0039-2`); until it is answered, SnatchArr writes nothing else to Stash, so an operator's manual edit inside Stash is not overwritten.
   - When SnatchArr ingests Stash scene/performer records into `adult_external_refs`, if Stash reports an `updated_at` timestamp newer than the last ingested `payload_sha256` and the field in SnatchArr is unlocked (`locked_by_user = FALSE`), SnatchArr detects that the local Stash record diverged from the upstream stash-box value matching Stash's `stash_ids`. Under the source priority list (or if Stash local edits are given priority above remote scrapers), SnatchArr promotes the Stash user edit or surfaces it in `adult_identity_suggestions` / `candidate_values_json` without clobbering it.

### 5. Merging duplicates

Duplicate entity resolution across all libraries obeys one universal invariant:

> **Invariant**: **Never merge two entities on name or title alone.** Display names (`Jane`, `Alex`, `The Office`, `Greatest Hits`) are ambiguous across both adult and general media catalogs. Every automated merge requires either an exact shared external identifier / normalized URL or a multi-signal composite score above the auto-merge threshold with zero hard conflict blockers.

#### 5a. Shipped adult favourite merger (`api/internal/adult/merge.go`)

Today, SnatchArr merges favourite performers (`MergePerformers`, `api/internal/adult/merge.go:39-78`) and studios (`MergeStudios`, `api/internal/adult/merge.go:80-115`) using deterministic union-find (`unionFind` in `api/internal/adult/merge.go:180-209`):

- Two candidate records merge **if and only if** they share an exact `(endpoint, foreign_id)` pair (`byLink` map, lines 53-62) **or** a canonical normalized URL (`byURL` map via `NormalizeURL`, lines 64-73).
- Candidates that share the same `CanonicalName` without a shared `(endpoint, foreign_id)` or normalized URL are **kept as distinct rows**, and `DisambiguateNames` (`api/internal/adult/merge.go:118-145`) appends `(Disambiguation)` or the first linked `(endpoint)` to prevent UI ambiguity.

#### 5b. Planned probabilistic matching engine, audit queue, and reversible splits ([ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md), [ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md))

[ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md) and [ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md) extend duplicate resolution to the full adult catalog (`adult_performers`, `adult_studios`, `adult_scenes`) using a Fellegi-Sunter log-likelihood scorer $S(a, b) \in [0, 100]$:

1. **Three confidence bands**:
   - **Auto-link ($S \ge 85$ and zero hard conflicts)**: Automatically links the incoming external reference to the existing canonical UUID and logs an `'auto_merge'` row in `adult_identity_events` (`before_state_json`, `after_state_json`, `signals_json`).
   - **Human audit queue ($60 \le S \le 84$)**: Creates a distinct canonical entity and inserts a `'pending'` row into `adult_identity_suggestions` ([ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md)) with the per-signal breakdown (`signals_json`) for operator review in the audit workbench ([ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md)). If the optional privacy-fenced outbound model connection ([ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md)) is enabled, the model may rank and explain $60 \le S \le 84$ candidates (`decided_by = 'model'`), but **may never auto-execute a merge** — every model recommendation stays in `adult_identity_suggestions` until an operator approves or rejects it.
   - **Auto-reject ($S < 60$ or any hard conflict)**: Keeps entities separate without queuing a suggestion.
2. **Hard conflict blockers (veto auto-merge regardless of score)**:
   - Explicit pair in `adult_identity_exclusions` (created whenever an operator rejects a suggestion or splits a previously merged entity).
   - Both records carry non-null `birthdate` values that differ on year, month, and day (and are not a single-axis month/day transposition).
   - Both records carry non-null `country` ISO codes that differ.
   - Both records already link to **different** `foreign_id` values on the **same** stash-box `endpoint`.
   - For scenes: duration difference $> 30\text{ s}$ (prevents trailer-to-full-scene merges) or `PHASH` fingerprint with `reports > 0` (fingerprint poisoning guard, [ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md)).
3. **Scene perceptual hash (`PHASH`) thresholds**:
   - Hamming distance $d(\text{phash}_a, \text{phash}_b) \le 4$ **plus** duration within $\pm 5\text{ s}$ **plus** `reports == 0`: eligible for auto-merge ($S \ge 85$).
   - Hamming distance $5 \le d \le 8$ with duration within $\pm 15\text{ s}$: routed to `adult_identity_suggestions` ($60 \le S \le 84$) for human review.
   - Hamming distance $d > 8$: ignored ($0$ points).
4. **Reversible split / undo**:
   - Every merge is recorded in `adult_identity_events`. Splitting an entity reverses the pointer reassignments from `before_state_json`, restores field values from `adult_field_provenance`, and inserts a permanent exclusion pair into `adult_identity_exclusions` so subsequent sync passes never re-merge the pair ([ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md)).

#### 5c. Numbered and studio-scoped performer identities (ADR-0037 (proposed))

Some studios and regional catalogs credit performers using studio-scoped or numbered identifiers (for example, a performer credited as a studio serial number or site-specific pseudonym on one studio's releases while holding a cross-studio canonical identity on StashDB or ThePornDB `PerformerSite.parent`). Per [ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md) and **ADR-0037 (proposed)**:

- `adult_scene_performers.credited_as` stores the per-scene or studio-scoped credit string verbatim without polluting `adult_performers.canonical_name`.
- Studio-scoped or numbered performer records are never merged solely because two studios use the same number or short given name; linking a studio-scoped performer to a global `adult_performers` row requires either an explicit upstream parent link (`ThePornDB` `PerformerSite.parent`, stash-box URL/ID link), a composite score $S \ge 85$ with corroborating scene co-appearances, or operator confirmation in the `adult_identity_suggestions` audit queue (ADR-0037 (proposed)).

#### 5d. General media duplicate prevention

For general media (`sonarr`, `radarr`, `lidarr`, `readarr`, `seerr`, `jellyfin`):

- Series and movies join strictly on `tvdb_id` (`api/internal/arrclient/resolve.go:54-58`, `api/internal/watchaware/source.go:89-96`) or `tmdb_id` (`api/internal/arrclient/resolve.go:49-53`), never on series or movie title.
- Music artists and albums in Lidarr and community requests ([ADR-0036](0036-community-requests-and-voting.md)) join on MusicBrainz `MBID` (`lidarr.Artist.ForeignArtistID`, `lidarr.Album.ForeignAlbumID`), using `lidarr.Artist.Disambiguation` when displaying homonymous artists.
- Book authors and works in Readarr-class managers join on `ForeignAuthorID` and `ForeignBookID` (`readarr.Author`, `readarr.Book`).

## Consequences

### Positive

- **Zero refresh oscillation**: Every field has one canonical owner (**Decision §1**) and a deterministic precedence rule (**Decision §4**). A refresh from a secondary scraper or a null upstream field can never erase a curated title, studio code, or user-locked attribute.
- **Path-independent identity joins**: Joining across systems via `(endpoint, foreign_id)`, TPDB `(UUID, _id)`, `tvdb_id`, `tmdb_id`, `MBID`, and `OSHASH`/`PHASH`/`SHA-256` (**Decision §2**) means file renames in `*arr` or Stash never orphan watch history, season gates, or audit exclusions.
- **Safe read-only defaults**: All background sync loops run on the elected leader via `gate.Provide[*T]()` and default to read-only pull (**Decision §3**), matching SnatchArr's existing `observe`-first contract in `watchaware` and `queue`.
- **Clean degradation**: Adopters running without Stash, without Whisparr, or with the `adult` module disabled get a well-defined ownership table (**Decision §6**) with zero dead dependencies.

### Negative

- **Provenance storage overhead**: Tracking `adult_field_provenance` (`candidate_values_json`, `value_sha256`, `locked_by_user`) per field increases PostgreSQL storage and write amplification during `adult.Ticker` sync passes compared to stateless overwrite.
- **Explicit operator review for ambiguous identities**: Prohibiting name-only merges (**Decision §5**) means homonymous performers or scenes in the $60 \le S \le 84$ grey zone remain separate until the operator approves or rejects them in `adult_identity_suggestions`.

## Alternatives considered

1. **Make Stash the single source of truth for all adult metadata and require Whisparr and Jellyfin to read from Stash**:
   - *Rejected*: Stash is optional in SnatchArr ([ADR-0027](0027-adult-module.md), [ADR-0032](0032-stash-own-or-integrate.md)); adopters running only Whisparr + Jellyfin would have no metadata hub. Furthermore, Stash's own schema stores a single value per field without multi-source `candidate_values_json` provenance or a Fellegi-Sunter audit queue ([ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md), [ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md)).
2. **Make Whisparr v3 the single source of truth and sync Stash and Jellyfin from Whisparr**:
   - *Rejected*: Whisparr v2 and v3 have incompatible internal schemas (`whisparr.Series`/`Episode` vs `whisparr.Movie`), neither tracks `OSHASH`/`PHASH` fingerprints or filesystem image sets (`adult_image_sets`), and Whisparr's metadata refresh overwrites local edits from a single upstream feed.
3. **Synchronize metadata across tools by having every tool read and write shared `.nfo` sidecar files on disk**:
   - *Rejected*: `.nfo` sidecars lack field-level provenance (`winning_source_ref_id`, `candidate_values_json`), cannot represent multi-endpoint `stash_ids` or `adult_identity_exclusions`, require read-write media mounts across every container, and are the primary vector for multi-tool overwrite loops today.
4. **Auto-merge performers and people whenever normalized display names match**:
   - *Rejected*: Directly causes the 415-weight performer collision failure mode in `.workingdir/evidence/stash-pain-research-20261026.md`, corrupting performer filmographies and triggering unwanted auto-snatches across unrelated performers who share a mononym.

## Verification plan

Every ownership and conflict rule in this ADR is verified by unit, property, and PostgreSQL integration tests under HISS-15 (positive, negative, and boundary cases) and HISS-20 (replayable fixtures that fail when the guard is removed):

1. **Planted conflicting refresh against a locked field (HISS-20 negative fixture)**:
   - *Setup*: Seed an `adult_scenes` row (or `adult_performers` row) with `title = "Curated Scene Title"`, `adult_field_provenance.locked_by_user = TRUE`, `decided_by = 'user'`, linked to a priority-`10` `stash-box` instance.
   - *Planted conflicting refresh*: Replay an upstream `stash-box` sync payload where the priority-`10` source returns `title = "Upstream Overwrite Title"`.
   - *Expected assertion*: `adult_scenes.title` remains `"Curated Scene Title"`, `adult_field_provenance.locked_by_user` remains `TRUE`, and `"Upstream Overwrite Title"` is recorded only in `adult_field_provenance.candidate_values_json`.
   - *Two-way replay proof*: Commenting out the `locked_by_user` guard in the reconciler causes the test to fail red (`title` overwritten); restoring the guard passes green.
2. **Non-empty guard boundary test**:
   - Replay a sync pass where a priority-`10` source returns `details = ""` (empty) and `duration = nil`, while a priority-`20` source previously populated `details = "Full synopsis"` and `duration = 1800`. Assert that neither field is overwritten by the higher-priority empty values.
3. **Priority ordering positive & negative tests**:
   - With `locked_by_user = FALSE` and strategy `overwrite`, replay conflicting non-empty values from priority `20` and priority `10` in both arrival orders (`10` then `20`, and `20` then `10`). Assert that both orders converge to the identical canonical value (`priority 10`) and identical `value_sha256`.
4. **Never-merge-on-name-alone & exclusion-blocklist tests**:
   - Replay two performers with identical `canonical_name = "Performer A"` and `disambiguation = ""` on different endpoints with no shared URL or external ID (`S = 15`). Assert two distinct UUIDs are created and `DisambiguateNames` suffixes their endpoint labels (`api/internal/adult/merge_test.go`).
   - Replay a split on a merged entity via `adult_identity_events`, then re-run the sync pass with identical upstream payloads; assert `adult_identity_exclusions` blocks re-merging ($S$ vetoed).
5. **Jellyfin `LockedFields` preservation test**:
   - In the `/api/v1/adult/jellyfin/*` and companion plugin contract tests ([ADR-0028](0028-jellyfin-plugin.md)), pass a fixture with `LockedFields = ["Name", "Overview"]` and verify that only unlocked fields (`Genres`, `People`, `Studios`, `ProviderIds`) are updated.
6. **Degraded-mode matrix tests**:
   - Run the `adult.Ticker` and Jellyfin metadata handler integration suite under three configurations: (a) Whisparr configured, zero Stash instances; (b) Stash configured, zero Whisparr instances; (c) `adult` module disabled (`IsEnabled(ctx, domain.ModuleAdult) == false`). Assert zero errors and verify that the fallback owner in **Decision §6** serves each field.

## Follow-up slices and milestones

Per `docs/roadmap.md`, follow-up implementation work is split across **0.9** (general media rows) and **0.10** (adult module rows):

### Why general rows land in `0.9` and adult rows land in `0.10`

- **Milestone `0.9 — Requests & voting`** (`docs/roadmap.md:107-118`, [ADR-0036](0036-community-requests-and-voting.md)) already introduces cross-library external ID resolution (`requests(external_source, external_id, target_instance_id, target_entity_id)`) across movies (`TMDb`), series (`TVDb`), music (`MusicBrainz`), and books (`OpenLibrary` / `Hardcover` / `Goodreads`) alongside existing `seerr` and `watchaware` loops. Completing the general-media external ID join contract and read-only boundary in `0.9` avoids introducing a second general-media lookup layer later.
- **Milestone `0.10 — Adult module completion`** (`docs/roadmap.md:121-131`, [ADR-0027](0027-adult-module.md), [ADR-0028](0028-jellyfin-plugin.md), [ADR-0031](0031-creator-content-fansdb.md), [ADR-0032](0032-stash-own-or-integrate.md), [ADR-0033](0033-adult-identity-and-model.md), [ADR-0034](0034-matching-engine-and-model-connection.md)) is the dedicated milestone on `docs/roadmap.md` for Whisparr wanted/snatch integration, multi-stash-box catalog normalization, filesystem image-set matching, Stash inventory hand-off, and the Jellyfin companion plugin. Grouping all `adult_*` schema migrations (`000040`, `000041`), provenance reconciliation, and degraded-mode adult handlers in `0.10` keeps the optional adult module self-contained behind its feature gate.
